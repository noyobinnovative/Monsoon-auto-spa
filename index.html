Questions

Can’t find your answer? Call or message us on WhatsApp.

How long does a wash take?

Express wash takes about 25 minutes, Monsoon Shine about an hour, and Full Detail 3 to 4 hours. Ceramic coating needs the car for a full day.

Do I need to book, or can I just drive in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking guarantees your slot. Weekends fill up fast, especially after heavy rain.

Is pick-up and drop really free?

It’s free with Full Detail within 5 km of our centre. For other packages it costs ₹199 within 10 km.

Will the wash damage my paint or PPF?

No. We use pH-neutral shampoo, filtered soft water and the two-bucket method with clean microfibre for every car.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the cancel button under “Your bookings” on this page, or message us. Changes are free up to 2 hours before your slot.

Do you offer monthly plans?

Yes. Four Monsoon Shine washes a month cost 15% less than paying each time. Ask at the counter or on WhatsApp.
